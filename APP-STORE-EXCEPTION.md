> **Draft.** This text has not been reviewed by anyone who knows licences. It must be reviewed
> before the first App Store release, and may change before then.

Drusite is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any
later version ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). The copyright holders also grant the additional permission
below, under section 7 of that licence. Contributions are accepted under the same terms (see
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md)), so the permission covers every contributor's code.

It covers every app store, not only Apple's, since they all impose the same kind of restrictions.
It is available to everyone, forks included, and anyone may remove it from their own copy, so it
can't be used to close anything.

term of this License, including section 13 (remote network
interaction), still applies in full. You may remove this permission
